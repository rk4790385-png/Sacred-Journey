# Sacred Journey – Project Vision

## Project Name

Sacred Journey

## Tagline

Plan. Travel. Experience. Preserve Your Spiritual Journey.

## Vision

Sacred Journey is a modern web platform that helps people plan, track, and preserve their spiritual pilgrimages across India.

Instead of simply listing temples, the platform enables users to organize pilgrimage routes, record travel memories, monitor progress toward completing sacred circuits, and maintain a lifelong digital journal of their spiritual journeys.

The application aims to combine pilgrimage planning, travel management, personal journaling, interactive maps, and progress tracking into one seamless experience.

## Mission

To create India's most comprehensive digital pilgrimage management platform that inspires devotees to explore sacred destinations while preserving every journey as a meaningful memory.

## Problem Statement

Many pilgrims currently rely on notebooks, spreadsheets, social media posts, and multiple travel websites to manage their journeys. There is no single platform where users can:

- Plan pilgrimage routes
- Track completed temples
- Record travel memories
- Upload pilgrimage photographs
- Monitor spiritual progress
- Estimate travel budgets
- View temple information in one place

Sacred Journey solves this problem by providing an all-in-one pilgrimage companion.

## Objectives

- Simplify pilgrimage planning.
- Help users organize multiple pilgrimage routes.
- Track completion of major pilgrimage circuits.
- Preserve memories through journals and photographs.
- Provide reliable temple information.
- Encourage users to complete spiritual journeys through progress tracking and achievements.

## Target Audience

- Individual pilgrims
- Families planning pilgrimages
- Spiritual travelers
- Temple explorers
- Devotees completing sacred pilgrimage circuits

## Scope (Version 1)

The first version of Sacred Journey will include:

- User authentication
- Personal dashboard
- Temple explorer
- Journey planner
- Interactive pilgrimage map
- Travel journal
- Progress tracking
- Profile management

Future versions may include AI itinerary suggestions, community features, multilingual support, weather integration, offline mode, and mobile applications.
